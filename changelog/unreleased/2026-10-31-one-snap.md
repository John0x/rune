- **Icons, vector shapes, fills, fitted images and text decorations share one sub-pixel placement rule (`t1193`).** Concentric elements in the same box no longer shift or change area against one another when scaled, anchored to a zoomed target, gliding on a spring, or swapping between `canvas` and `icon`. Icons pad their atlas masks with a 1-texel transparent border and sample filtered from the box's centre and exact scaled side whenever fractional, scaled or turned, while keeping direct whole-pixel texel loads at unscaled rest; point-sized `Shape`s centre in their snapped rect; zoomed `El::anchored` overlays open their own pixel-snap layer so their resting fills snap to whole device pixels; and text glide now runs on both axes (`1/6 px` horizontal and vertical phases on top of each glyph's resting pen, so headings above `33⅓ ppem` glide across as smoothly as body copy, along with pill icons, spans, list markers and carets). Output at unscaled rest is unchanged.
