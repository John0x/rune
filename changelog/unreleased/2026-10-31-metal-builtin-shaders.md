- **Rune's Metal backend loads every built-in pipeline precompiled, on macOS and iOS.** Backdrop and layer blur, video, transparent windows, springs, `rune-fx` and `rune-light` take their functions from `builtin_native.metallib` (`builtin_native_ios.metallib` and `builtin_native_ios_sim.metallib` on iOS, where the uber shader comes from it too) instead of naga and Metal's MSL compiler. On a cold shader cache on macOS, a first layer blur takes 45 ms instead of 727, `FxRenderer::new` 1.26 s instead of 3.63 and `LightRenderer::new` 127 ms instead of 1.39 s. On the iOS simulator, a cold first frame takes 243 ms instead of 430. Warm, `FxRenderer::new` takes 1.2 ms instead of 32.5 on macOS, because a built-in shader's WGSL is no longer parsed. App shaders, such as `rune-fx` custom fills and `Compositor` WGSL, compile at runtime as before. The library adds about 540 KB to a macOS or iOS binary built with `metal-backend`, which is on by default. After changing a built-in shader or its bind group layout, run `scripts/precompile-metal-shaders.sh` on a Mac. Until then, that pipeline compiles at runtime, and rune-light's `builtin_shaders` test fails under `RUNE_GPU_BACKEND=metal`.
