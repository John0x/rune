- **Mermaid diagrams stream in.** A ```` ```mermaid ```` fence still arriving in a `Markdown` is now a diagram drawn from its complete lines, not a code block that turns into one when it closes: with `Markdown::streaming` on, nothing shows until the first lines read, then each new node fades and grows in, each edge or message draws on from its source to its target with its arrowhead landing last, frames fade in, and what's already there glides to its new place. Closing the fence moves nothing. A finished diagram still shows whole the frame it mounts. Each node, edge and frame is now its own element keyed by what it is (a flowchart node by its Mermaid id), and `rune_mermaid::render_streaming` and `rune_mermaid::may_draw` are new. Underneath, `Transition::draw()` and `Shape::drawn(t)` trace a shape's strokes on along their length, each fill fading in as the line reaches it; under Reduce Motion the shape is drawn whole.
