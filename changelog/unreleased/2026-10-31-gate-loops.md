- **A virtual list keeps the rows still on screen mounted while a fold glides open over them, and the motion gate stops counting one-shots and toggled lists as loops.** A list windowed by the open fold's full height while it still glided, so a view that ran again mid-glide unmounted the rows under it at full opacity two frames before the fold covered them. The window now also keeps the rows drawn inside the viewport on the last frame, within an overscan of it. In `rune::motion_gate::check`, a phase that rests at 1 (a `.sequence` that played out, such as a one-shot fade) is no longer a loop: it isn't checked for `loop-jump`, and what it does after isn't hidden. A track without a phase loops only when it turns back on its own, not within half a second of an input, so a list toggled again and again no longer hides every finding in it. `loop-jump` also catches a loop back from a blink of up to 0.1 s off its rhythm, and an opacity dip that one input starts and another reverses is no longer a `flicker`.
