- **Text glides with its box instead of stepping a pixel at a time.** Pixel snapping glides a moving box between device pixels, but its glyphs still rounded their baselines to whole pixels, so a card drifting a few pixels on a slow spring drew its words jumping a whole pixel against its edges. A gliding box's text now lays out from the box's nearest whole pixel and draws the rest of the distance with glyph masks rasterized at quarter-pixel vertical phases, hinted as at rest. In a 2× card drifting 5 px, the ink's offset from the card's edge varied by about 1 px before and 0.25 px now; it was already within 0.15 px across. Each glyph adds at most three masks while it moves. Text at rest, including a glide's last frame, draws byte for byte as before. Icons and list markers still sit on whole pixels.
