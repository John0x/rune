- **A streamed surface or `pixels_fit` image can stick to a corner, and hold still while it waits for a resized frame.** `El::surface_anchor(Align, Align)` chooses which edges `Fit`'s spare space and `Fill`'s crop are measured from (centred by default, so nothing moves), and `El::surface_unscaled(true)` draws one texel per device pixel instead of scaling to the box. With both, the last frame of a resized panel stays pinned top-left: a bigger box shows a gap on the right and bottom, a smaller one crops those edges, until the frame at the new size arrives. `SurfaceFit` is unchanged.
