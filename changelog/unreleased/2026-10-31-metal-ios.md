- **Rune's native Metal backend (`metal-backend`) runs on iOS.** An app embedding an `embed::LayerHost` opts in with `rune::prewarm_gpu_on(GpuBackend::Metal)` before its first host, or `RUNE_GPU_BACKEND=metal`; wgpu stays the default. The surface takes a UIKit view or a `CAMetalLayer`, picks iOS's MSL version, and keeps the drawable timeout (iOS has no occlusion check or display-sync switch). On the iOS simulator the backend's tests pass and a scrolling list renders the same pixels as wgpu's, with about 36% fewer instructions and CPU per frame; launch and first-frame time are unchanged, since iOS still compiles shaders at run time on both backends. A new `embed_cost` example measures it.
