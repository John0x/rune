- **A streaming `Markdown` no longer flashes markup at its tail.** With `Markdown::streaming` on, an emphasis, strong, strike or code span that's open draws as already styled and never shows its `**`, `_`, `~~` or `` ` ``: `**Settings ›` draws "Settings ›" in bold until the closer arrives. Markers at the very end (`and **`) wait for the next character, which says whether they open, close or are text. Only an opener at a word's start counts, so `2 * 3`, `snake_case`, `~5` and a URL's underscores stay as written, and a `* ` that becomes a bullet never shows as a star. A trailing `| File` line now draws nothing until it's a table (two cells, or a delimiter row) or the next line shows it isn't, so a table's header no longer lays out as a full-width paragraph and then shrinks. A finished document parses exactly as before.
