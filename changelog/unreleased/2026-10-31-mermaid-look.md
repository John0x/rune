- **Mermaid lines, outlines and arrowheads no longer brighten where they overlap, and the default diagram look is quieter.** Rune draws each stroke segment as its own capsule, so a translucent line blended twice at every joint: curves got beads, rounded node corners came out brighter than their sides, and a line got brighter under its own arrowhead (in dark, a line of luma 154 reached 207 at joints and heads). `rune-mermaid` now gives every translucent stroke, and every arrowhead in a line's colour, the opaque colour it would show over the fills beneath it (the card, a subgraph tint, a node), so overlaps paint the same colour twice; tints and bands keep their alpha. This applies to every diagram type. The default look changes with it: the card is the page colour with a hairline instead of a lighter slab, nodes are `layer_01` with a 0.2 outline, lines are 0.42 of the text colour, edge and message labels are muted, subgraph outlines are 0.7 of a node outline (they were 0.45 of the text colour, brighter than nodes), subgraph titles are medium weight instead of semibold, and a cylinder's top is a shade off its side so it reads without an outline. `DiagramStyle::neutral` and `MarkdownStyle::from_kit` both change; goldens with diagrams move in colour only. Keep `diagram_surface`'s fill opaque: ink is flattened over it.
